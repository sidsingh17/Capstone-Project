AI-Powered Supply Chain Risk Intelligence Assistant
Overview
Global supply chains involve suppliers, warehouses, transportation networks, inventory systems, and logistics operations that continuously generate operational data. Organizations often face challenges such as supplier delays, shipment disruptions, inventory shortages, transportation bottlenecks, and unexpected demand fluctuations.
Supply chain managers frequently spend significant time analyzing operational risks because information is distributed across procurement systems, shipment records, warehouse data, vendor reports, and logistics platforms.
Managers frequently investigate situations such as:
"Supplier delivery delays are increasing for critical components." 
"Port congestion is impacting shipment schedules." 
"Warehouse inventory is approaching stockout levels." 
"Transportation costs increased unexpectedly across specific regions." 
"Demand spikes are causing fulfillment bottlenecks." 
Traditional monitoring systems rely heavily on threshold alerts and manual analysis, making proactive risk management difficult.
The system should help operations teams retrieve historical supply chain incidents, identify emerging risks, recommend mitigation strategies, and generate explainable operational intelligence.

Objective
The objective of this project is to build an AI-powered supply chain risk intelligence assistant that enables operations teams to investigate supply chain disruptions using natural-language queries and intelligent risk analysis.
The system should retrieve historical operational incidents, correlate logistics signals, detect supply chain anomalies, and generate explainable risk insights to improve operational resilience and business continuity.
